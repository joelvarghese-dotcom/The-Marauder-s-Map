Explore the Campus

A residential campus in the Sahyadri ranges, with spaces to learn, live, eat, exercise and connect. Here is a quick guide to the facilities highlighted by the university.

📚

Libraries

Two libraries support different academic programmes, with print collections, journals, e-resources, study areas and online catalogue access.

Central Block · 1st Floor Management Block · 2nd Floor
🍽️

Cafeterias & Food

Multiple dining options across campus offer a range of meal and snack choices. Food outlets and timings may change.

Campus-wide
🏠

Residences

Residential facilities support campus living and student community life. Contact the hostel/residence office for room, visitor and maintenance procedures.

Residential areas
🏃

Sports & Fitness

Indoor and outdoor sports facilities, a fitness centre and organised sporting activities support an active campus experience.

Sports facilities
🩺

Health & Counselling

Health services and counselling support are available. For urgent assistance, contact campus security or the designated campus support team.

Medical Point · Central Block · 2nd Floor*
🚌

Transport

University transport options include services within Lavasa and selected Pune routes. Check current schedules and booking rules with the transport office.

Schedule-dependent
🛡️

Campus Security

Campus security support and surveillance operate across the campus. Save the current campus emergency contact number from official student communication.

Campus-wide
🌿

Learning Commons

Informal spaces around the Central and Management Blocks encourage peer learning, group discussion, socialising and collaborative work.

Both blocks

*Medical Point location is listed in the university handbook. Confirm current access and operating hours locally.
